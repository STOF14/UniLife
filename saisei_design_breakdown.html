Palette philosophy
Zero chromatic hues used for brand identity. The entire palette is warm neutrals — pulled from natural materials (stone, sand, timber, ash). This is a deliberate Wabi-sabi reference: imperfection and simplicity over colour saturation. No accent colours, no call-to-action reds or greens. The only contrast comes from light vs dark tonality.

Typography rules
Mixed-case on headlines — neither all-caps nor sentence case consistently. Large display text is tracked tightly (negative letter-spacing). Navigation uses numbered prefixes "(1) Home" — a Swiss typographic device. Kanji appears as small-cap metadata above section titles, always in parentheses with romanized translation below. CTA links use italic or underline animation on hover rather than colour change.
03
Layout & Spacing
Grid system
Full-bleed with generous margins
Content sits in a centered column with wide gutters. Sections use asymmetric two-column splits — image left, text right or vice versa at a roughly 55/45 ratio.
Vertical rhythm
Long-scroll, section-per-concept
Each page section occupies near full-viewport height. Sections are separated by large whitespace rather than dividers or rules.
Hero layout
Full-bleed image + overlaid type
Hero imagery fills the viewport. Brand name sits center or bottom-left in large display type. "Scroll" indicator bottom center with animated caret.
Navigation
Minimal fixed top bar
Left: EST 2022 marker. Center: numbered nav links. Right: hamburger or contact shortcut. Nav expands to full-screen overlay with contact details and legal links.
Section anatomy — consistent across all pages
Kanji superscript — small parenthetical label "(den tou) · Tradition" above every major section. Left-aligned. Acts as a chapter marker.
Large section heading — H2 level, Fragment typeface, sometimes stacked with the kanji reading.
Body paragraph — Aeonik, 16-17px, long-measure, muted grey on off-white or off-white on dark.
CTA link — repeated twice (animated marquee style: "About Us · About Us" duplicated), underline or arrow suffix, no button borders.
04
Imagery & Visual Direction
Source
Midjourney V6.1
All imagery is AI-generated. Extremely high fidelity — looks indistinguishable from real architectural photography.
Mood / colour grade
Muted, warm, desaturated
Low saturation. Warm light sources (golden hour, overcast diffused). Deep shadows. Film-like tonal contrast.
Subject matter
Japanese minimalist interiors & exteriors
Shoji screens, stone courtyards, round windows, cherry blossom, timber details, zen gardens, clean concrete.
Crop & framing
Architectural editorial
Wide establishing shots for heroes. Detail crops for section imagery. Symmetrical or near-symmetrical compositions. Frequent use of negative space in-frame.
Horizontal scroll image strips
Several sections use a horizontal auto-scroll strip of 5–6 images that moves left continuously on a loop. Images are equal-sized, close-cropped portraits or detail shots. This is used on Home, Service, and Sustainability pages to break up text-image alternation and add kinetic energy.
05
Animations & Interactions
Page enter / scroll-triggered reveals Core
Text and images fade + translate-up on scroll into view. Staggered delay between heading, body, and CTA. Clean easing, ~600–800ms.
Horizontal marquee / ticker CTA Signature
"About Us · About Us" links run as duplicated horizontal scrolling text on a loop. Seen on multiple pages. No pause on hover on some instances. Subtle speed — not frenetic.
Auto-scrolling image strip Signature
A row of images scrolls left infinitely at a slow, meditative pace. Multiple strips used — some scroll left, some may reverse. Speed is unhurried, matching the zen brand tone.
Scroll-driven hero parallax Core
Hero image moves at a different rate from the page content on scroll, creating depth. Combined with a "scroll" indicator that animates (bouncing caret or progress bar).
Project carousel / slider Section-specific
Home page project section cycles through 3 featured projects with prev/next controls. Current project's image crossfades or slides. Counter (1/3) updates accordingly.
Navigation overlay expand Core
Clicking the menu icon transitions to a full-screen dark overlay containing contact info, nav links (numbered), social links, legal, office hours. Likely a clip-path or opacity + transform reveal.
CTA hover — link underline draw Core
Text links animate an underline from left to right on hover. Used on "About Us", "See Project", "Get in touch" links throughout. No colour change — motion only.
Accordion (Service process steps) Section-specific
On the Service page, the 5-step process section uses a click-to-expand accordion. "More info / Close" toggle. Inner content slides open/closed with ease-in-out.
Number counter / large decorative text Signature
Section index numbers are displayed very large in a light weight, behind or adjacent to content — decorative typographic framing. Awards listed with large numbered index.
Image hover zoom (project cards) Core
Project thumbnails scale subtly on hover (scale ~1.04) within a fixed container. "See project" text appears as an overlay or slides up. Smooth transition, ~300ms.

Numbered 1–10 list of award wins. Large index number left, award name + category + year right. Divider lines between entries. Clean editorial format.
07
Brand Language & Philosophy System
Kanji / Japanese concept system
Every major section is anchored by a Japanese philosophical concept, presented as: (romanization) above, and the English translation below. Examples: (den tou) Tradition · (Kaku SHIN) Innovation · (Ken chiku) Architecture · (Fu ku mu) Service · (setsu zoku) Connect. These act as section identifiers and deepen the cultural brand positioning. They are styled small, low contrast, and never compete with the headline.
Japanese philosophy quotes
Each page ends or includes a longer Japanese philosophy quote section — a short concept name in parentheses with a multi-sentence explanation of how it informs Saisei's work. E.g. "Ichi-go ichi-e", "Chousensha", "Shizen no Junkan". These appear against a full-bleed architectural image at the bottom of major sections.
Voice / Tone
Measured, poetic, reverent. No urgency language. No superlatives. Philosophical rather than commercial.
Logo / Wordmark
SAISEI wordmark in Fragment. No logomark — the word is the mark. SVG kanji characters used as decorative project identifiers.
Cultural positioning
Tokyo-based. Wabi-sabi aesthetics. Zen philosophy. Japanese modernism x sustainability. Clearly referencing SANAA, Kengo Kuma territory.
